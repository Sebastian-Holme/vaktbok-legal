Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Norsk

Who we are

Vaktbok is provided by MilesTech AS. Contact: post@milestech.no.

For the logbook content, the organisation using the app (the employer) is the data controller and MilesTech AS is the data processor. For the user account itself (email and sign-in), MilesTech AS is the data controller.

What we store

  • Account: email address, a user ID, the name you pick from the logbook and your role (staff, leader or administrator).
  • Logbook content: what the leader imports from the organisation's logbook – for example resident codes, arrival and departure times, medication times, driving lists, staffing, responsibilities and comments.
  • Records: who marked a task or shared chore as done, and when, plus an audit log of imports and changes.

What we don't do

  • No tracking, no advertising and no third-party analytics.
  • We do not sell or share data with others.
  • The app does not prescribe medication and does not replace the organisation's clinical system.

Purpose

The data is used to sign you in, give you access to your unit, show your shift, the tasks and changes that concern you, and document who did what.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database service) in the EU (Frankfurt) and protected by access rules so only approved members of the same unit can access it. An encrypted copy is kept on the phone, and the app can be locked with Face ID.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on the phone. By default the lock screen shows no resident details.

Retention

Logbook days are deleted automatically after 14 days (adjustable by the organisation). The audit log and shared-chore history are deleted after 180 days. Account details are kept until the account is deleted.

Your rights

You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Slett kontoen min). Requests for access, correction or deletion of logbook content go to your employer. You can also contact us at post@milestech.no. You have the right to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
